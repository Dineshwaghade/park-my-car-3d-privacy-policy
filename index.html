Park My Car 3D

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Park My Car 3D is a free car parking game by Waghade Studio, paid for by ads. It has no in-app purchases, no analytics and no sign-in, and it never asks who you are. Your progress and settings stay on your own phone. Two things use the internet: the ads, served by Google AdMob (see below), and the optional online leaderboard for the Endless mode, which stores an anonymous player ID, the nickname you choose and your best Endless scores.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your progress: the stars for each level, which levels are unlocked, which missions (Beat the Clock, No Scratch) you have finished, and your best Endless runs.
  • Your coins and the paint, rims and upgrades you have bought or equipped in the garage.
  • Your settings: sound volumes, control layout, camera view and the cabin light switch, and which one-time tips you have already seen.
  • Your leaderboard nickname, and any Endless score that is waiting to be sent because you were offline.
  • The anonymous sign-in token the leaderboard uses (see below).

All of this is kept in the app’s own private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The online leaderboard

The Endless mode has a world leaderboard. It is optional: the whole game, including Endless, works without it, and nothing is sent until you open the Endless screen or finish an Endless shift. To make it work, the game uses Supabase, a hosted database service, in its Tokyo (Japan) region.

Anonymous player ID
The first time the leaderboard is used, the game creates an anonymous account for this install. It is a random ID with no email, phone number, name or password attached. The game keeps the matching sign-in token on your phone.
Nickname
The name shown next to your scores. It starts as a random “Driver1234” and you can change it in the game. It is visible to everyone who opens the leaderboard, so do not use your real name or anything personal. Nicknames with offensive words are refused.
Endless scores
For each map you play in Endless: your best number of parks, how long that shift took, when the best was set and when you last played that map.

That is everything the game sends to the leaderboard. Like most online services, Supabase’s servers may briefly record technical details of each request, such as your IP address and the time, for security and to keep the service running, under Supabase’s own privacy policy (supabase.com/privacy).

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on some menus, an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never while you drive), and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a reward, such as double coins or extra time in Endless. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in your phone’s settings);
  • your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps, and diagnostic data about ad delivery.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices. If you decline, you may still see ads that are not personalised. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. Google handles this data under its own policies: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

How long leaderboard data is kept

  • A score is deleted automatically 180 days after you last played that map. Every Endless shift you finish keeps your scores on that map alive.
  • An anonymous player account is deleted once it is more than 180 days old, has not signed in for 180 days and has no scores left.
  • To have your leaderboard data deleted sooner, email us with your nickname (see Contact) and we will remove it.

Security

  • Everything the game sends to the leaderboard travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Players cannot change the leaderboard directly. Scores go through checks on the server that keep only plausible results and acceptable nicknames.
  • Only Waghade Studio can manage the leaderboard database.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own. Waghade Studio does not receive your advertising ID or any of the ad data Google collects.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.
  • No selling of personal information. Beyond the ad data Google collects as described above, nothing is shared with anyone.
  • No access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Permissions

INTERNET
Used for the ads, the online leaderboard and showing this policy inside the game. The game itself plays offline.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
Lets the game check whether you are online before loading an ad.
AD_ID (advertising ID)
Added by Google’s ads library so AdMob can use your device’s advertising ID, as described under Ads.

Reading this policy in the game

Settings → Privacy policy loads this page from GitHub Pages, which is run by GitHub. Your phone makes one ordinary request for the page, and GitHub may log your IP address when it does, under its own privacy statement. If the page cannot be reached, the game shows the copy of this policy saved inside it instead.

Children

Park My Car 3D is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has put personal information in a leaderboard nickname, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. The game always shows the latest version when you are online.
